2. What Hybrix does

Hybrix is an app for creating strength and endurance training plans and sending them to Garmin devices. Coaches can optionally be invited by users to view data and create plans.

3. Data we process

  • Account data: email address, display name, role (athlete or coach).
  • Training content: workouts and plans you or your coach create.
  • Garmin data: after you connect your Garmin account, we receive your completed activities (for example type, duration, distance, heart rate, pace) through the Garmin Connect Developer Program APIs. We also store the authorization token for your Garmin connection.
  • Coach relationship data: which coach you have invited, and the status of that access.

Activity and heart-rate data can reveal health information. We therefore process it only with your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). Account data is processed to provide the service you requested (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

4. Purposes

We use your data only to provide Hybrix: building and syncing workouts, showing your progress, generating personalized plans and suggestions, and enabling coach collaboration at your request. We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising and do not share it with other users.

5. Coach access

A coach can see your training data and create plans for you only if you invite them. Access is limited to the coach you choose, enforced by row-level security in our database, and can be revoked by you at any time in the app. Plans created by a coach are sent to your Garmin device only through your own authorized connection and only after you accept them. Coaches have no access to your Garmin account or credentials.

6. Service providers (processors)

  • Supabase – database, authentication and storage. Region: [EU/Frankfurt – bitte anpassen].
  • Anthropic (Claude API) – generates training plans and analyzes your own training data to give feedback and plan adjustments. Only the data of the requesting user is sent, and only when that user (or their invited coach, for that user's plan) requests it. [Bitte Anthropics aktuelle API-Datenbedingungen prüfen und hier korrekt wiedergeben, z. B. zur Nutzung für Modelltraining.]
  • Garmin Ltd. / Garmin Connect – you connect your own Garmin account; Garmin processes your data under its own privacy policy.

Where data is transferred to countries outside the EU/EEA (for example the USA), we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision. [Bitte mit den Providern prüfen.]

7. Retention and deletion

We keep your data as long as your account exists. You can disconnect Garmin at any time, which deletes the stored Garmin token and stops data retrieval. You can delete your account and all associated data in the app or by emailing us; we delete it without undue delay. If you delete Garmin data on your side or revoke authorization, we delete the corresponding data we hold on request.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection, and to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting prior processing). You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, for example the one responsible for your place of residence.

9. Security

Data is transmitted encrypted (TLS), access is controlled by authentication and row-level security, and tokens are stored server-side only.
