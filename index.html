Hybrix

Strength and endurance training in one plan, sent straight to your Garmin device.

Hybrix is a mobile app (React Native) for athletes who combine running, cycling and gym training. You build a structured training plan, or let your coach build it for you, and the workouts are synced to your Garmin device through Garmin Connect.

Status: in development (private beta with a small group of friends).

How it works

1. Build your plan

Create structured workouts: intervals, tempo runs, bike sessions, or gym workouts with sets, reps and rest. AI assistance can suggest a plan based on your goals.

2. Connect Garmin

Link your Garmin account with Garmin's official OAuth flow. You stay in control and can disconnect at any time.

3. Train

Accepted workouts appear on your Garmin device. Completed activities come back into Hybrix so you can follow your progress.

Optional: invite your coach

You can invite your personal coach. They can view your training data and create plans for you. Sharing is opt-in, limited to the coach you choose, and you can revoke it at any time. Coaches have no access to your Garmin account.
